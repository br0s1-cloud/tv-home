Connect Spotify

Links your Spotify to Brosi Home so the TV can show your playlists and albums from your top artists, and start them playing. Your Mac must be on the TV's Wi-Fi for step 2.

Step 1. Sign in to Spotify and click Agree.

Brosi Home can read your playlists and top artists, and control playback. It can't change your library, playlists or account.
Step 2. Copy this into Terminal and press Return. It finds your TV and hands it the sign-in. 

    
 

    Treat this like a password: don't share it. It stops working if you disconnect Brosi Home in your Spotify account settings, and Spotify asks you to sign in again about every 6 months.
